I recently graduated from high school (Abitur) and have a deep-seated passion for full-stack software development and hardware engineering.

In the last months, I bridged the gap between software and hardware by designing a custom PCB for an ESP32-C3-based water detector, a project that required mastering SMD soldering, hardware debugging, and RF measurement using a VNA. Building on that, I designed a wearable convention badge around a bare ESP32-S3 chip, featuring a 4-color e-paper display, 48 individually addressable LEDs and a full battery system with charger and fuel gauge, all configurable over WiFi.

Additionally, I am developing a centralized management platform for Minecraft servers that integrates real-time analytics and cross-server player statistics as well as a management website for my school.

I am currently studying Applied Computer Science and look forward to refining my ability to build complex, creative, and technically robust systems.
